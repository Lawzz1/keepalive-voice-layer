Before you start

  • Use the laptop you'll demo on: its built-in speakers and mic. Headphones hide the leak.
  • Set the volume to what you'll use in the demo.
  • Sit or kneel where the rescuer would be. Keep the room as it will be.
  • Run the steps in order. Step 4 needs your voice.
